🗑️ How to delete your Math Owl account

Option 1: in the app (immediately)

  1. Open Math Owl and sign in.
  2. Tap ⚙️ My account.
  3. At the bottom, under 🗑️ Delete account, enter your password, type the word DELETE and confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, write from the account’s email address to aur.taureci@gmail.com with the subject “Delete Math Owl account”. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • the account (email and password);
  • the profile: in-game name, country, picture;
  • points, scores and contest results;
  • your chat messages and your private conversations;
  • the list of players you blocked.

What is kept

  • Safety reports sent about or by this account, for at most 12 months, to protect children.
  • The in-game name may remain in the ranking of a contest that has already finished, with no other data.
