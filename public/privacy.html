🔒 Privacy Policy – Math Owl

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short, for parents: Math Owl is a maths game for children in 2nd and 3rd grade. We keep only the data the game needs (the in-game name, the account email, points and, if you use the Math Owl Chat app, messages). We do not sell data, we currently show no ads, and you can delete it at any time together with the account.

Apps this policy covers

  • Math Owl – the maths game (islands, lessons, contests, leaderboard).
  • Math Owl Chat – the separate app for chat and video calls, with the same account and database as the game.
  • National Assessment · 2nd grade – the separate app with the practice tests. It has no account and sends no data: the best score is kept only on the device.

1. Who is responsible for the data

Math Owl is created and run by a private individual in Romania (the app developer). For any question or request about your data, write to aur.taureci@gmail.com.

2. What data we use and why

DataWhy
In-game name (may be a nickname) and countryTo show you in the leaderboard, contests and Math Owl Chat.
Email address and passwordFor signing in and resetting the password. The password is stored encrypted by Google Firebase; we never see it.
Profile picture (optional)Only if you choose one; other players see it in chat and the leaderboard.
Points, scores, completed levels, contestsTo keep your progress and build the leaderboard.
Chat messages and call history (who, when, how long) – only in Math Owl ChatSo the chat works. Call audio and video go directly between the two devices and are not recorded.

Parental consent (ticked when creating the account and before chat)As a record that the parent agreed.

The legal basis is providing the service you asked for (the game) and, for children, parental consent. We do not use the data for profiling, personalised advertising or automated decisions.

3. Children and parental consent

The game is made for children. The account must be created by a parent or guardian, or with their consent (in Romania, parental consent is required for children under 16). Before entering Math Owl Chat for the first time, the child confirms that a parent knows and agrees. We recommend using a game name that is not the child’s full name.

4. Chat safety

The Math Owl game no longer has a chat. Chat and calls are only in the separate Math Owl Chat app, where:

  • Other players are not shown in a public list; a child can only be found by searching for their name.
  • Bad words are automatically replaced with ***, and phone numbers and email addresses are hidden.
  • Any player can be reported (⚠️) or blocked (🚫). A blocked player can no longer write or call.
  • Reports are reviewed by the administrator, who can delete messages or accounts that break the rules.

5. Who helps us store the data

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.) – accounts and database. Data may also be stored on servers outside the EU, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses).
  • Vercel – website hosting.
  • Google STUN servers – help connect calls; they do not receive call content.
  • flagcdn.com – country flag images.
  • If someone posts a YouTube or Vimeo link in the chat, the video is shown by that service, under its own rules.

Ads

The Math Owl apps currently show no ads. If we turn ads on, they will be served by Google AdSense (Google Ireland Ltd.), we will update this page first, and the ads:

  • are tagged as child-directed, so they are non-personalised: Google does not use the child's interests or history and does no remarketing;
  • appear only on the island pages, labelled “Advertisement”, never during an exercise;
  • may use technical data (IP address, browser type, cookies or identifiers needed for serving, measurement and fraud prevention).

In the EU, a Google consent message will appear on the first visit, where you can choose what you allow. Details: how Google uses data from partner sites. We do not give Google the child's name, email, photo or scores.

6. How long we keep data

For as long as you have an account. When you delete the account, the account, profile, picture, points, scores, your messages and private conversations are deleted. Safety reports are kept for at most 12 months. Unused sessions without an account may be deleted at any time. Progress and the chosen language are also saved on the device (in the browser); you can remove them by clearing the site’s or app’s data.

7. Your rights

You have the right to know what data we hold, to correct it, delete it, receive it in a portable format, object to processing and withdraw consent. Write to aur.taureci@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Romanian Data Protection Authority (www.dataprotection.ro) or to the authority in your country.

8. Deleting the account

In the app: ⚙️ My account → 🗑️ Delete account. Or see the account deletion page.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top and, for important changes, announce it in the app.
